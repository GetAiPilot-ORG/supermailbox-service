# Sales & Support workspace

Mode: Operate. A single Metabull admin uses this surface repeatedly to find the next customer action, classify registered GetAiPilot users, schedule follow-ups, and resolve basic support work without entering Mail Operations.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The workspace is an action queue before it is a report. It refuses the passive CRM dashboard made from equal KPI cards and instead ranks overdue follow-ups, high-intent unreviewed people, onboarding drop-offs, and urgent tickets by what needs attention now.

**OWN-WORLD:** It extends SuperMail's warm mineral ground, white working surfaces, graphite ink, sage selection and compact hairline controls. Dense tables, disciplined status chips, tabular numerals, and one consistent Lucide stroke make state legible without decorative chrome.

**STORY:** The admin enters, sees whether GetAiPilot data is fresh, clears the highest-risk signals, opens a person's unified record, records the outcome, and leaves every active lead with a next step. Funnel and pipeline evidence support that work rather than displacing it.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow Sales rail frames a full-width command strip with title, 30-day range, sync state, and refresh action. Directly below, a five-cell metric ledger precedes a dominant two-column field: a ranked Needs attention queue at roughly two-thirds width and a compact activation funnel at one-third. The signature interaction is opening any signal into the same contextual lead drawer without losing queue position.

**FORM:** Signal Queue, fifth on the grounded structural list and the assigned lead from surface seed `1cdec4e6`. People, Pipeline, Follow-ups, and Support are subordinate work views that preserve the same drawer and action vocabulary.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: Preserve all Mail Operations routes and behavior. CRM storage is additive and isolated. Desktop target is 1440px, mobile target is 390px, WCAG AA applies, and reduced motion removes nonessential transitions.
